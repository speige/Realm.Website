Why Realm

Made for custom-map energy

Jump into Arcade chaos or host your own — less lobby waiting, more creating and playing, with tools that keep older maps alive.

  • Multiplayer mayhem

    Support for dozens of players in the same match and hundreds of units on screen

  • Instant feel

    Server authoritative with local prediction makes de-sync an issue of the past while still maintaining competitive integrity.

  • Self-Hosted

    Zero risk of public servers being shutdown and breaking the game

  • Legacy maps still play

    Compatibility mode rolls the engine back so older maps keep working when the core updates.

  • AI-assisted creation

    Map editor ships with an AI assistant — prompt your way to a working map.

  • Open assets

    Share models, textures, and scripts. Remix community work and grow the Arcade together.

Creator suite

Terrain, units, code — live-test as you build

Sculpt maps, place props, edit unit stats, and hot-reload to test. Editor includes an AI agent when you need help.
